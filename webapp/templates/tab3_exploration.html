Trends over time, the categories that dominate the caseload, and where crime concentrates.

Is crime rising or falling?

Neither — the series is flat. The fitted trend is well under one crime per year against a mean in the low 220s, and every year sits within a few percent of that mean. The operational reading is that total volume is stable, so resourcing decisions should be driven by where and when crime happens rather than by any expectation that the overall caseload is shrinking.

Insight

The two rankings tell different stories. Ranking by raw volume points at large, populous areas; ranking per 10,000 residents promotes small areas whose residents face a far higher individual risk. Patrol allocation should follow the volume ranking — that is where the calls are — while prevention and community programmes should follow the per-capita ranking, because that is where residents are most exposed.
